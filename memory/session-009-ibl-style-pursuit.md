## Context
After studying IBL-sorter (`/scratch/ap7151/_REFERENCE/ibl-sorter`), the project's raw `omega x sigma` convolution pursuit was compared with IBL's pipeline:

- IBL detects on **locally-whitened, destriped, re-phased data** projected onto template spatial factors (a matched filter), not on raw voltage. Threading every torch math kernel with a global spatial whitening rotation is a big surgery.
- The small, correct, faithful step is the **noise-weighted `Delta chi^2` acceptance gate** that was already derived in `docs/noise_weighted_pursuit_acceptance.tex`: `Delta chi^2 = alpha_hat^2` (Eq. 109), replacing/augmenting the `min_captured_fraction` heuristic.
- IBL detection summary (from reference): destripe (300 Hz+ order-3, k-filter spatial), local ZCA whitening 32-nearest channels, spkTh=-6 isolated-peak template bootstrap, then mexMPnu8 matched-filter pursuit with Th=6 (learn) / Th=3 (final) in whitened unit-noise units, isolation margin eloss = best-vs-second-best score, subtractive peeling up to 500 iters, kriging 10x subsample alignment. Kilosort analog lives at `/scratch/ap7151/_REFERENCE/Kilosort`.

## Spatial-lattice finding (2026-08-26)
- The anomalous first fit `chunk_00_row_0011` is not explained by alpha scaling: its selected normalized spatial footprint has `g[peak channel 10]=0.99485` and only `0.034-0.056` on the other real contacts. Its refined source is approximately `(10.62, -0.98, 0.00)` um in the local frame, i.e. essentially directly above channel 10, with `sigma=1` um. Thus alpha correctly scales all channels, but the selected lattice profile itself is nearly a one-contact delta function.
- The current production solver permits this degeneracy. `src/maths.py` defines `VOXEL_LO=(-150,-150,0)` and its shrinking-grid refinement clamps candidates to that bound, producing an implicit 1 um full grid that includes `z=0`.
- UnitMatch SLT's basic solver uses the same normalized monopole formula and includes `sigma=1` um, but its discrete lattice is `x,y` uniform over `[-150,150]` and `z=geomspace(1,300)`: no source at the contact plane. It also stays on that coarse lattice rather than refining every source to a 1 um voxel. This is a material difference, not merely a threshold-crossing detector difference.
- Solver distinction: UnitMatch assigns a footprint by the energy over a shared Q-dimensional temporal subspace and reconstructs each spike with a free linear combination `v_s^T A`. The current pursuit localizer uses a fixed learned bank and hard-selects one temporal atom plus a scalar alpha. Both normalize spatial footprints and use exact discrete candidate argmax; UnitMatch's temporal flexibility is greater, but the immediate one-channel pathology is the current `z=0` plus `sigma=1` spatial candidate.
- Next experiment before changing alpha or temporal orientation: impose a positive depth floor in both coarse/refined search (and likely remove/raise the 1 um sigma profile), regenerate first-fit PNGs, and compare the spatial footprint and per-channel Delta chi2 for the same rows. Do not change the scientific model beyond that constraint until this visual comparison is available.
- SpikeInterface's monopolar triangulation independently confirms `z >= 1` um as a standard physical bound, but does not establish it as sufficient to prevent one-contact fits. It fits per-channel PTP/energy/peak-voltage features to `alpha / sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2 + z^2)` (no independent sigma), constrains `z >= 1`, and its default peak localizer also radially clips non-decreasing feature amplitudes. Its default log-penalty weakly prefers larger z. At `z=1` on a 20 um pitch, a neighboring predicted amplitude is still only about 1/20 of the nearest-contact value, so a nearly single-channel result remains admissible. This is a distinct post-detection localizer, not a waveform reconstruction/pursuit solver.

## Kilosort4 detection + denoising (reference at `/scratch/ap7151/_REFERENCE/Kilosort`)
- KS4 ("9f8e705") pipeline: high-pass + median-CAR → local ZCA whitening (32 nearest channels, ZCA `Wrot[j,ix]=first row`) → drift → **universal-template detection** → clustering → **learned-template pursuit** → final clustering/merge → save_to_phy.
- Detection gate = **matched-filter projection amplitude threshold**, NOT chi^2. Universal pass: `conv1d(whitened X, 6 unit templates)` → 5 spatial Gaussian sizes x 6 templates → max over |A| → dedup over 100 nearest template centers + local max over ±20 samples → `As > Th_universal = 9` (whitened-sigma units). Learned pass: `Cf = relu(B)^2/nm`, gate `sqrt(Cf) > Th_learned=8`, local max ±61 samples, up to `max_peels=100` subtractions per batch.
- Denoising comes from **(a)** template = cluster-mean of spike PC features (`Wall` (n_cl, Nchan, n_pcs=6), averaged twice), **(b)** low-dim temporal PC reconstruction (`templates = Wall otimes wPCA`), **(c)** residual+template feature construction (`xfeat = Xres @ wPCA + amp * Ucc`), **(d)** alignment (peak rolled to nt//2) + merging. KS4 has NO `lam`, NO median-smoothing, NO KS2.5 nrank=3 spatial SVD.
- Exported amplitudes = `norm(tF)`, positions = norm-weighted channel mean within 100 um, duplicate removal 0.25 ms, CCG refractory merge, whitening_mat_inv for display.
